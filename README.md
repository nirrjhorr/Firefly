# Firefly

> *"Fireflies shine in the dark. In the darkness, the light that they provide shows you the beauty that cannot be experienced otherwise."*

---

## The Vision Behind the Name

Firefly was born from a simple realization: when life feels overwhelming, you don't need a harsh floodlight demanding that you be happy, productive, or cured. Bright lights blind tired eyes. 

Instead, you need a firefly — a small, gentle glow in the dark.

In life’s heaviest moments — 2:00 AM anxiety, low-energy numbness, sensory overload after a grueling day, or the quiet ache of loneliness — that soft light doesn't try to banish the night all at once. It simply illuminates the very next step, revealing a quiet, grounded beauty and calm that can only be found when we meet darkness with gentle compassion.

---

## Why We Are Building This

### The Reality of Acute Distress
When someone is experiencing acute emotional overwhelm, their cognitive bandwidth collapses. The prefrontal cortex — responsible for executive planning, multi-step decisions, and self-regulation — is functionally exhausted.

At this exact moment, traditional mental health applications fail:
- **They demand too much energy:** Clinical 15-question mood inventories, complex 30-day programs, or therapist matching screens feel insurmountable when getting out of bed feels hard.
- **They induce guilt through gamification:** Streaks, badges, and missed-day notifications create psychological pressure. When a streak inevitably breaks during a user's worst week, the breach triggers shame that compounds the original distress.
- **They demand internet and personal data:** Studies show that nearly 80% of mental health apps share user data with third parties. Yet, people in vulnerability or distress deserve absolute privacy and sanctuary.
- **They are quickly abandoned:** Clinical engagement research (*Baumel et al.*) shows that 30-day retention across mental health apps drops to 3.3%, and standalone breathing apps collapse to 0%. If an app requires weeks of effort to feel helpful, it fails the person who needs comfort *right now*.

## Our Approach: How Firefly Works

Firefly is built around five human-first, trauma-informed commitments:

### 1. Gentle Light, Low Stimulation
Every screen is quiet and deliberate. Built on a deep, soothing midnight canvas with soft sage accents, Firefly never flashes, never startles, and respects system reduced-motion preferences. There are no sudden sounds, aggressive colors, or visual noise.

### 2. Value in Under 2 Minutes
Every intervention is designed to deliver a perceptible nervous-system shift within 120 seconds. We design with the understanding that *any visit may be the only visit*. You don't have to unlock levels or complete modules to feel grounded.

### 3. One Step at a Time (Zero Decision Paralysis)
When executive function is low, choosing between ten options is effectively having zero options. Firefly's gentle check-in evaluates your current state and offers **one recommended next step**. You are never left navigating a maze of choices.

### 4. Zero Guilt — No Streaks, No Badges, No Shame
There are no streak counters. No notifications warning you that you've missed a day. No celebratory confetti that trivializes the non-linear reality of healing. The only metric of progress is presence: *"You showed up."* Come when you need it; step away whenever you're ready.

### 5. A 100% Offline Sanctuary
Your thoughts, your mood logs, and your safety plans belong solely to you. Firefly has **no user accounts, no cloud databases, no analytics trackers, and no network calls**. Everything stays encrypted on your device. It works on a flight, in a subway, in remote areas, or in the middle of the night without signal.

## What We Are Building: The Core Modules

Firefly is actively taking shape module by module, prioritizing emotional safety and immediate clinical utility:

### 🛡️ The Anchor: Safety & Crisis Support *(Completed)*
- **Stanley-Brown Safety Plan**: The clinical gold-standard crisis prevention protocol, accessible offline at all times.
- **Rapid Panic Blank Mechanism**: Long-pressing the emergency trigger instantly blinds the screen to neutral black and purges memory state for instant user safety and privacy.
- **Persistent SOS Overlay**: An accessible, single-tap safeguard that hovers quietly across the entire experience.

### 🧭 The Compass: Affect Check-In Engine *(Completed)*
- **30-Second Check-In**: Five moon-phase tiles (Heavy, Low, Here, Light, Open) and peaceful, non-numeric sliders.
- **Deterministic Guidance Engine**: A pure, mathematical routing engine that translates current mood and energy into a single, compassionate recommendation without clinical diagnostic labels.

### 🌿 The Breath: Respiration & Sensory Grounding *(Completed)*
- **Evidence-Backed Cyclic Sighing**: Implements the clinical protocol (4s inhale / 8s extended exhale) proven to outperform standard mindfulness in restoring autonomic nervous system balance (*Balban et al., 2023*).
- **Organic Bloom Visualizer**: A custom-crafted, expanding radial bloom that guides breath pacing effortlessly, even through peripheral vision, with full reduced-motion accessibility.
- **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Mode**: A guided sensory journey anchoring you to your physical surroundings (5 sights, 4 sensations, 3 sounds, 2 scents, 1 taste) with penalty-free skipping for sensory differences.
- **Offline Ambient Soundscapes**: Gapless audio loops (calm synthesizer drone, gentle rain, grounding acoustic chimes, or complete silence) with synchronized tactile haptic cues.

### 👣 The Spark: Tiny Steps Behavioral Activation *(In Active Development)*
- A curated offline library of 20+ low-barrier micro-actions (e.g., *drink cool water*, *open a window for fresh air*, *stretch your hands*).
- Specifically crafted to interrupt depression inertia and anxiety paralysis without demanding high energy.

### 🕯️ The Haven: Private Reflection & Unsent Letters *(Upcoming)*
- Space for expressive writing, unburdening thoughts, and unsent letters with an optional auto-delete feature for peace of mind.

### 🌙 The Gentle Progress *(Upcoming)*
- Non-punitive, warm weekly reflection celebrating simple moments of presence rather than competitive streaks.
