# Firefly

> *"Fireflies shine in the dark. In the darkness, the light that they provide shows you the beauty that cannot be experienced otherwise."*

---

## The Vision Behind the Name

Firefly was born from a simple realization: when life feels overwhelming, you don't need a harsh floodlight demanding that you be happy, productive, or cured. Blinding lights only dazzle tired eyes.

Instead, you need a firefly — a small, gentle glow in the dark.

In life’s heaviest moments — 2:00 AM anxiety, low-energy numbness, sensory overload after a grueling day, or the quiet ache of nighttime loneliness — that soft light doesn't try to banish the night all at once. It simply illuminates the very next step, revealing a quiet, grounded beauty and calm that can only be found when we meet darkness with gentle compassion.

### 1. Affect Labeling: Hitting the Amygdala's Brakes
* **The Finding:** Research by Matthew Lieberman and colleagues demonstrates that putting feelings into words (*affect labeling*) immediately dampens amygdala reactivity and activates the right ventrolateral prefrontal cortex (RVLPFC).
* **How Firefly Uses It:** Instead of clinical 15-question diagnostic forms, Firefly opens with five intuitive moon-phase selectors (Heavy, Low, Here, Light, Open) and peaceful energy sliders. By simply naming their state in under 30 seconds, the user activates neurological "brakes" on acute distress without cognitive strain.

### 2. Cyclic Sighing: Rapid Autonomic Regulation
* **The Finding:** In a landmark remote randomized controlled trial published in *Cell Reports Medicine* (Balban et al., 2023), Stanford researchers compared daily 5-minute breathwork protocols with mindfulness meditation. **Cyclic sighing** (two physiological inhales followed by a prolonged, unhurried exhale) produced significantly greater improvements in positive affect and physiological calming (reduced respiratory rate and heart rate) than other breathing techniques and standard meditation.
* **How Firefly Uses It:** Firefly's Respiration Engine guides users through a clinical 4-second inhale and 8-second extended exhale cadence. It pairs this cadence with an organic, expanding bloom visualizer and dual-frequency haptic cues, stimulating the vagus nerve to restore Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and parasympathetic balance in as little as 60 seconds.

### 3. Single-Session Interventions (SSIs): Designing for the Reality of Drop-Off
* **The Finding:** An umbrella review across 415 trials and over 40,000 participants (*Schleider et al., Annual Review of Clinical Psychology 2025*) demonstrated that self-guided digital Single-Session Interventions (SSIs) produce meaningful, lasting reductions in anxiety and depression ($SMD = -0.25$). Conversely, real-world data (*Baumel et al., 2019*) reveals that median 30-day retention across mental health apps is only 3.3%, and standalone breathing apps drop to 0.0%.
* **How Firefly Uses It:** Firefly operates under the design rule that **each visit may be the only visit**. Every module is self-contained and delivers measurable value in **under 2 minutes**, eliminating multi-week dependencies.

### 4. Behavioral Activation (Tiny Steps): Breaking Inertia
* **The Finding:** Meta-analyses encompassing over 10,000 participants show that Behavioral Activation (BA) yields substantial effects in treating depression ($g = 0.83$ vs. inactive controls), matching full Cognitive Behavioral Therapy in efficacy by interrupting cycles of avoidance and lethargy.
* **How Firefly Uses It:** For users paralyzed by low energy or depressive inertia, Firefly's *Tiny Steps* library offers curated, sub-2-minute micro-actions (e.g., *take a slow sip of water*, *open a window for cool air*, *stretch your fingers*). It restores agency without cognitive friction.

### 5. Stanley-Brown Safety Planning: Gold-Standard Crisis Guardrails
* **The Finding:** The ED-SAFE 2 trial published in *JAMA Psychiatry* (2023) demonstrated that collaborative safety planning decreases subsequent suicide-related acute healthcare visits or deaths by over 40% (adjusted $OR = 0.57$).
* **How Firefly Uses It:** Firefly embeds the complete, offline Stanley-Brown Safety Plan directly into the application. It includes an emergency persistent SOS trigger and a rapid **Panic Blank mechanism** (long-pressing instantly blanks the display and purges state), ensuring crisis safety is always accessible in zero taps with zero network latency.

### 6. Somatosensory Grounding (5-4-3-2-1): Disrupting Catastrophizing Loops
* **The Finding:** Cognitive overload traps attention in ruminative thought loops. Shifting attention outward across external sensory modalities (vision, touch, sound, smell, taste) interrupts prefrontal distress loops and activates sensory cortices, reconnecting the individual to physical reality.
* **How Firefly Uses It:** Firefly's guided 5-4-3-2-1 exercise provides low-stimulation sensory prompts with tactile check-offs and penalty-free skipping, specifically supporting individuals with sensory differences (such as anosmia).

### 7. Why We Refuse Gamification: The Danger of Streaks
* **The Finding:** Psychological research on gamification in mental health indicates that streak counters exploit loss aversion, establishing an unspoken obligation. When a user in deep distress misses a day — the very time they are most vulnerable — the broken streak induces guilt and feelings of personal failure, accelerating app abandonment.
* **How Firefly Uses It:** **No streaks. No badges. No confetti.** Firefly only tracks gentle, non-judgmental presence: *"You showed up this week."*

## Our Approach: How Firefly Works

Firefly is built upon five trauma-informed design principles:

### 1. Gentle Light, Low Stimulation
Every screen is quiet and deliberate. Built on a deep, soothing midnight canvas (`#111518`) with soft sage accents (`#4A7862`), Firefly never flashes, never startles, and strictly adheres to system reduced-motion preferences.

### 2. Value in Under 2 Minutes
Every intervention is designed to deliver a perceptible nervous-system shift within 120 seconds. There are no levels to unlock or assessments to pass before you receive comfort.

### 3. One Step at a Time (Zero Decision Paralysis)
When executive function is depleted, multiple choices overwhelm. Firefly's gentle recommendation engine evaluates your current state and surfaces **one primary action**. Secondary alternatives remain quiet and unhurried.

### 4. Zero Guilt, Zero Shame
You are never penalized for stepping away. Firefly sends no manipulative "We miss you" notifications. Come when you need warmth; step away whenever you are ready.

### 5. A 100% Offline Sanctuary
Studies show that nearly 80% of mental health apps leak data to third parties. Firefly has **no user accounts, no cloud databases, no analytics trackers, and no network calls**. Everything stays encrypted on your device.

## What We Are Building: The Core Modules

Firefly is taking shape module by module, prioritizing emotional safety and immediate clinical utility:

### 🛡️ The Anchor: Safety & Crisis Support *(Completed)*
- **Stanley-Brown Safety Plan**: The clinical gold standard, accessible offline at all times.
- **Rapid Panic Blank Mechanism**: Long-pressing the emergency trigger immediately blacks out the screen and purges state for instant user safety and privacy.
- **Persistent SOS Overlay**: An accessible, single-tap safeguard hovering quietly across the app.

### 🧭 The Compass: Affect Check-In Engine *(Completed)*
- **30-Second Check-In**: Five moon-phase tiles (Heavy, Low, Here, Light, Open) and non-numeric sliders.
- **Deterministic Guidance Engine**: A pure, mathematical routing engine that translates current mood and energy into a single, compassionate recommendation without diagnostic labels.

### 🌿 The Breath: Respiration & Sensory Grounding *(Completed)*
- **Evidence-Backed Cyclic Sighing**: Implements the clinical protocol (4s inhale / 8s extended exhale) proven to restore autonomic nervous system balance.
- **Organic Bloom Visualizer**: A custom `CustomPainter` radial bloom that guides breath pacing effortlessly, even through peripheral vision, with full reduced-motion accessibility.
- **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Mode**: A guided sensory journey anchoring you to your physical surroundings with penalty-free skipping for sensory differences.
- **Offline Ambient Soundscapes**: Gapless audio loops (calm synthesizer drone, gentle rain, grounding acoustic chimes, or complete silence) with synchronized tactile haptic cues.

### 👣 The Spark: Tiny Steps Behavioral Activation *(In Active Development)*
- A curated offline library of 20+ low-barrier micro-actions (e.g., *drink cool water*, *open a window for fresh air*, *stretch your hands*).
- Specifically crafted to interrupt depression inertia and anxiety paralysis without demanding high energy.

### 🕯️ The Haven: Private Reflection & Unsent Letters *(Upcoming)*
- Space for expressive writing, unburdening thoughts, and unsent letters with an optional auto-delete feature for peace of mind.

### 🌙 The Gentle Progress *(Upcoming)*
- Non-punitive, warm weekly reflection celebrating simple moments of presence rather than competitive streaks.
