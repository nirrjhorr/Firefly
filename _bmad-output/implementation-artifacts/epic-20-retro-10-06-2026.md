## 1. Executive Summary

Epic 20 tackles executive dysfunction, mental fatigue, ADHD overwhelm, and task-induced anxiety. Grounded in Cognitive Load Theory (*Sweller 1988, 2011*) and neurobiological models of prefrontal cortex fatigue (*Arnsten 2009; Snyder 2013*), Firefly now delivers a serene, trauma-informed focus suite:
1. **Brain Dump:** Offloading unstructured mental clutter from fragile working memory into an unjudged holding vault.
2. **Three Priorities ("Rule of 3"):** Enforcing an unyielding cognitive throttle of at most 3 micro-intentions at any one time, protecting against choice paralysis and defeatism.
3. **Serene Focus Companion:** A calm, non-punitive focus interval timer (5, 10, 15, 25 minutes) with smooth circular visualizer, gentle play/pause/reset, optional ambient sound accompaniment, and zero productivity guilt, scoring, or streak pressure.

## 3. Retrospective Insights

### What Went Well
- **Zero Guilt Alignment:** Strict adherence to Firefly Principle 3 ("Zero Guilt — No Streaks, No Shame"). When the timer completes or pauses, the UI offers quiet validation rather than gamified productivity scores.
- **Strict Component Reusability:** Built entirely with canonical widgets (`FireflyNavHeader`, `FireflyCard`, `FireflyButton`), guaranteeing visual and tactile harmony.
- **Immediate Distress Connection:** Direct link from `RightNowModal` ensures users in acute cognitive overwhelm can bypass multi-step check-in and immediately start offloading tasks.
