## 3. Retrospective Insights

### What Went Well
- **Pure Dart Domain Cleanliness:** Separating icon resolution into presentation helpers preserves clean architecture and enables 100% of standalone tests to execute headlessly in pure Dart.
- **Zero-Network Invariant:** The entire 70-practice library, search, filtering, and assets function completely offline without external queries.

### Process Improvements
- Ensure domain models never take dependencies on Flutter UI (`IconData`, `Icons`) to maintain test harness isolation.
