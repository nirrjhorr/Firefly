## 1. Executive Summary

Epic 19 addresses one of the most clinically potent drivers of depressive rumination and acute anxiety: harsh internal self-criticism and shame. Operationalizing Kristin Neff's empirical 3-component self-compassion framework (*Neff 2003, 2023; Kirby et al. 2017*) alongside cognitive defusion, Firefly now offers users an immediate, trauma-informed sanctuary to soften internal judgment and externalize unhelpful cognitive loops without shame or pressure.

---

## 2. Key Delivered Capabilities

1. **Kristin Neff 3-Pillar Self-Compassion Model:**
   - Sequential somatic and cognitive progression:
     1. *Mindfulness:* Acknowledging the pain of this moment without suppression or amplification.
     2. *Common Humanity:* Recognizing that struggle and imperfection are universal shared human experiences rather than isolated failure.
     3. *Self-Kindness & Soothing Touch:* Placing a warm hand over the heart/belly and offering gentle, loving reassurance.
2. **Interactive Thought Untangler:**
   - 3-step structured externalization:
     1. *Externalize the Critic:* Naming the harsh story without shame.
     2. *Human Reality Check:* Acknowledging that fatigue and stress amplify perceived catastrophe.
     3. *The Compassionate Friend Reframe:* Writing the exact words of kindness one would offer to a beloved friend.
3. **Apple-Inspired Serene Sanctuary Interface (`CompassionScreen`):**
   - Mounted at `/home/compassion` and root modal `/compassion`.
   - Dual-mode toggle between "Self-Compassion Break" and "Thought Untangler".
   - Pulsing soothing aura visualizer synchronized with gentle pacing.
   - Persistent SOS shield overlay on all steps.
4. **Universal Routing & Library Catalog Integration:**
   - Seeded `act_self_compassion_break` and `act_thought_untangler` in `assets/data/curated_activities.json`, bringing total library to 73 evidence-backed practices.
   - Integrated into `RightNowModal` with immediate anchor: *"I am being too hard on myself"*.
   - Updated `ActivityCategory` and `RegulationGroup.expression` taxonomy.
