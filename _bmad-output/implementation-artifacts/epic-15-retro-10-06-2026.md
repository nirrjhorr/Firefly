## 2. Key Delivered Capabilities

### 1. Ambient Audio Mixer & Sleep Wind-Down (Story 15.1)
- **Multi-Track Acoustic Layering:** Interactive 6-channel mixer allowing simultaneous playback and individual volume scaling of offline sound textures (Gentle Rain, Deep Brown Noise, Warm Hearth, Night Crickets, Ocean Waves, Night Chimes).
- **Restful Zero-Decision Presets:** 5 evidence-calibrated presets ("Deep Blanket", "Rainy Hearth", "Summer Night", "Ocean Drift", "Calm Shelter") for effortless relaxation when decision fatigue is high.
- **Logarithmic Fading Sleep Timer:** Mathematical volume attenuation curve ($f(t) = \ln(1 + 9 \cdot (t / 300)) / \ln(10)$) applied over the final 300 seconds of a 15–60 min timer, preventing sudden silence awakenings.
- **Low-Stimulation Sanctuary UI:** Dedicated `AmbientAudioMixerScreen` with `#0A0D0F` background, warm amber `#E5B870` accents, tactile volume sliders, mute toggles, and master play/pause.
- **Routing & Seeding:** Direct routes at `/ambient-mixer` (modal with SOS overlay) and `AppRoutes.ambientMixer` (`/home/ambient-mixer`), query parameter handling in `AppRoutes.soundscapes?mode=mixer`, and launcher banner in `SleepTimerSelector`.
- **Validation:** 100% verified via `test/features/soundscapes/verify_ambient_audio_mixer_standalone.dart`.

### 2. Social Connection & Cooperative Activities (Story 15.2)
- **Cooperative Connection Modes:** Evidence-informed connection activities from Activity Architecture Section 3.6:
  - *Parallel Quiet:* Shared co-presence without small talk or performance pressure.
  - *Appreciation Micro-Note:* Tiny gratitude message explicitly releasing reply obligations ("no need to reply").
  - *Cooperative Games & Shared Puzzles:* Suggestions for non-competitive collaborative games (The Mind, Hanabi, Forbidden Island, shared jigsaws).
  - *Low-Barrier Shared Task:* Body doubling during mundane daily tasks.
- **Extended Invitation Templates:** 7 calibrated reach-out templates reducing friction for isolated users.
- **Tactile UI Section:** `CooperativeActivitiesSection` integrated into `LonelinessComfortScreen` with expandable detail drawers and one-tap SMS invitation triggers.
- **Empirical Cognitive Behavioral Evidence:** Fully coupled with `GuessVsRealitySheet` and `LonelinessComfortDao` SQLite persistence to systematically dismantle negative social expectations over time.
- **Validation:** 100% verified via `test/features/loneliness_comfort/verify_cooperative_activities_standalone.dart`.
