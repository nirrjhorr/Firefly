## 3. Retrospective Insights

### What Went Well
- Pure-Dart domain logic (`SlidingGridState` and `ConstellationPattern`) completely decoupled from UI framework, ensuring rapid mathematical verification and testing.
- Parity-safe shuffling (`shuffleSolvable`) eliminates parity deadlock bugs common in random tile generation.
- Full compliance with Firefly non-gamification rules: no move limits, no star ratings, no high score boards, no error buzzers.

### Process Improvements & Technical Debt
- Standalone verification pattern continues to provide lightning-fast, high-confidence regression testing across the entire suite.
- Font assets and Vosk model integrated in the prior correction sprint remain verified and stable.
