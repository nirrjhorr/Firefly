## 1. Executive Summary

Epic 22 introduces the evidence-based **Awe Walk Protocol** (`RM-05`), operationalizing the randomized controlled trial findings of **Virginia Sturm et al. 2020** (*Emotion*, UCSF Memory and Aging Center). Sturm et al. demonstrated that regular outdoor walks instructing participants to direct their attention outward toward vastness, scale, and natural novelty significantly reduce daily emotional distress, interrupt circular ruminative thinking, and foster the soothing "small self" perspective.

Firefly now provides a serene, low-stimulation walking companion with paced duration presets (5, 10, 15 minutes), curated sensory prompt decks, a sensory gratitude anchor, and post-session settledness feedback logging to Firefly's on-device personal effectiveness engine.

## 2. Key Delivered Capabilities

1. **Protocol State Machine & Domain Models (`AweWalkPhase`, `AwePrompt`, `AweWalkSession`):**
   - 4-phase sequential state machine (`preparation`, `vastness`, `smallSelf`, `gratitude`, `complete`).
   - 5 curated observational cues grounded in Sturm et al. 2020:
     1. *Panoramic Horizon* (Panoramic gaze beyond immediate path)
     2. *Canopy & Sky Depth* (Looking up into branches and cloud architecture)
     3. *Micro-Wonder & Geometry* (Intricate miniature moss and leaf veins)
     4. *Acoustic Openness* (Distinguishing multi-layered soundscapes)
     5. *The Small Self Comfort* (The grounding relief of humility within a vast ecosystem)
   - Immutable `AweWalkSession` with duration presets, elapsed time, progress fraction, active prompt tracking, and JSON serialization.

## 3. Retrospective Insights

### What Went Well
- **Direct Operationalization of Clinical RCTs:** Following Sturm et al. 2020 directly gave the feature rigorous scientific grounding and structured simplicity.

- **Seamless Integration with Epic 21:** Completed Awe Walks automatically log to `ActivityEffectivenessLog`, feeding into the user's personal regulation affinity profile.
