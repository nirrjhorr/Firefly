## 3. Key Architectural & Product Decisions

1. **SOS Panic Button Hierarchy:**  
   In shell navigation (`MainShellScaffold`), individual activity screens disable internal SOS buttons (`showSosOverlay: false`) to avoid stacking two floating buttons. When rendered as full root modals over `_rootNavigatorKey`, `showSosOverlay: true` ensures panic blanking is always one tap away.
2. **Behavioral Experiment Insulation:**  
   Prediction data in the "Guess vs. Reality" engine is kept entirely local, anonymous, and unlinked to contact identity in aggregate summaries, completely preserving user dignity and safety.
3. **Multi-Media Vault Cryptographic Hygiene:**  
   Deleting items in Hope Box physically unlinks the media file from app-private documents directory, crypto-wipes memory buffers, and updates the encrypted store.
4. **Immediate Distress Fast Paths:**  
   The 13 distress anchors in `RightNowModal` provide 1-tap entry into appropriate regulation engines without requiring questionnaires, cognitive calculations, or surveys.
