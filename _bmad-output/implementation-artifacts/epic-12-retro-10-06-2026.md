## 3. Key Architectural & Product Decisions

1. **Decoupled Pure-Dart Domain Geometry:**  
   In Story 12.3, geometrical path generation was implemented using pure Dart `LabyrinthCoord` value objects, ensuring domain logic remains 100% decoupled from Flutter UI framework dependencies (`dart:ui` / `package:flutter`), enabling fast standalone testability and clean architecture compliance.
2. **Organic Parasympathetic Pacing (~0.1 Hz):**  
   The somatic visualizer aura operates at a calm 8-second cycle (~0.1 Hz), gently matching resting parasympathetic and vagal nerve activation rhythms without flashing or distracting high-contrast shifts.
3. **Non-Judgmental Tracing Freedom:**  
   Unlike arcade-style maze games, the meditative labyrinth has zero wall collision punishments, zero wrong turns, and zero countdowns. The canvas encourages slow, unhurried finger tracking, with an optional toggle to clear or retrace at will.
4. **Offline Activity Catalog Alignment:**  
   13 new activities across Nature, Somatic Mindfulness, and Labyrinth were added to `assets/data/curated_activities.json` and mirrored in `ActivitySeedingService` emergency fallbacks.
