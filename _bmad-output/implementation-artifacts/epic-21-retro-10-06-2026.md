## 1. Executive Summary

Epic 21 closes Firefly's core self-regulation feedback loop (RM-AA-19 / FR-16). Grounded in **Bandura's self-efficacy theory**, **Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA)** personalization, and **Schleider & Baumel's adaptive single-session intervention principles**, Firefly now computes on-device mathematical affinity scores for activities based on user-reported post-activity shifts (-2 to +2 ratings).

Rather than static recommendations, Firefly learns what actually settles the user's nervous system across specific emotional states ("When I feel anxious, cyclic sighing helped +2", "When my mind is scattered, 3 priorities helped +2"). All computation is 100% on-device, deterministic, private, and encrypted in SQLite with zero cloud telemetry.

## 2. Key Delivered Capabilities

1. **Bayesian/Laplace Damped Affinity Engine (`PersonalEffectivenessEngine`):**
   - Pure Dart mathematical service that normalizes ratings into `[-1.0, 1.0]`.
   - Incorporates sample count damping: `dampedScore = (normalizedAvg * (N / (N + 1.0)))` preventing single noisy ratings from destabilizing recommendations while converging smoothly with repeated practice.
   - Suppresses unhelpful practices (ratings < 0) from top calming recommendations.
   - Tracks positive, neutral, and negative session outcomes.
2. **Personal Regulation Profiles (`PersonalRegulationProfile` & `ActivityAffinity`):**
   - Groups top calming practices by specific affect/emotional states (Anxious, Overwhelmed, Scattered, Low Energy).
   - Computes total moments of self-care and overall calming rate without gamified scoreboards or guilt.
   - Full JSON serialization and roundtrip support.
3. **State-Matched Recommendation Personalization (`RecommendationEngine`):**
   - Evaluates affect states against personal affinities and clinical priors.
   - Enriches recommendations with evidence badges (`personalizedReason: "Previously helped you feel more settled"`).
   - Reorders alternative suggestions to surface the user's proven top calming options first.
   - Gracefully promotes an alternative to primary recommendation when proven personal relief is exceptionally high (> 0.45) and baseline is untested.
   - Falls back seamlessly to default clinical priorities on cold start (zero ratings).
4. **Affect Result Card Evidence Badging (`AffectResultCard`):**
   - Calm, illuminated chip displaying the personal reason with `Icons.auto_awesome_rounded`.
   - Expanded action type icons for all 15 core regulation modalities.
5. **Personal Sanctuary Screen (`PersonalProfileScreen` at `/home/profile` & `/profile`):**
   - Calm Apple-inspired interface with `FireflyNavHeader`, `FireflyCard`, `FireflyEmptyState`, and `SosOverlayButton`.
   - Categorized cards displaying top practices per state, times practiced, and average shift.
   - Privacy guarantee banner highlighting 100% on-device encrypted storage with 1-tap "Reset Sanctuary Learning" confirmation dialog.
   - Accessible from both `CheckInScreen` and `ActivitiesScreen`.
