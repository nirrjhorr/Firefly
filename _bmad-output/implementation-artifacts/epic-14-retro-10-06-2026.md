   - Catalog seeding in `curated_activities.json` and `ActivitySeedingService` under `ActivityCategory.emotionalExpression`.

2. **Cognitive Defusion Prompts Suite (Story 14.2):**
   - **Leaves on a Stream (`leavesOnStream`):** Custom-painted tranquil woodland river canvas with gentle ripples and floating autumn leaves carrying user thoughts smoothly downstream and off-screen.
   - **Thought Cloud Dissolve (`thoughtClouds`):** Twilight sky canvas with ambient stars and drifting clouds that dissolve into soft mist when tapped or released.
   - **Thought Labelling & Distance (`thoughtLabeling`):** Structured 3-tier linguistic defusion step progression creating non-judgmental psychological distance ("I am failing" → "I notice I am having the thought that..." → "I notice my mind is giving me a story... and I am here observing it").
   - 12 curated starter prompts (`kCuratedDefusionPrompts`) alleviating typing burden for fatigued users.
   - Standard "That's enough for now" exit path triggering `EffectivenessFeedbackSheet`.
   - Modal (`/defusion`) and shell (`/home/defusion`) routing with SOS overlay compatibility.
   - Seeded in `curated_activities.json` and fallback catalog under `ActivityCategory.cognitiveDefusion`.

## 3. Retrospective Insights

### What Went Well
- Complete separation of pure-Dart domain logic (`DefusionMode`, `DefusionThought`, `DefusionSessionState`) ensures instant standalone verification and zero reliance on heavy native compilation during unit testing.
- Decoupling `JournalTtlOption` from presentation controllers into domain models cleaned up legacy technical debt and prevented transitive dependency leaks.
- 100% adherence to Firefly non-gamification design tenets: zero scoreboards, zero timers, zero failure alerts, low-stimulation dark palettes (`#111518`), and touch targets ≥ 56dp.

### Process Improvements & Technical Debt
- Standalone verification suites continue to execute in seconds with zero build-runner latency.
- Total activity count in `curated_activities.json` reached 70 curated self-regulation activities spanning all 16 canonical categories.
