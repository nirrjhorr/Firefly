## 1. Licensing Philosophy & Risk Mitigation

Firefly is built as an offline-first, privacy-critical mental wellbeing companion. Because the app distributes binary packages on mobile operating systems (Android APK/AAB, iOS IPA), any asset bound by restrictive non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND), or viral share-alike (SA) clauses creates severe operational liabilities.

To eliminate all legal friction:
1. **No Non-Commercial Only (NC) Assets:** All curated audio assets permit commercial redistribution, ensuring that any future freemium, donation, or enterprise mental health deployment does not violate original copyright grants.
2. **No Share-Alike (SA) or GPL Licenses:** Excludes copyleft contagion that could force the entire client application or proprietary UI modules into open copyleft releases.
3. **No Unverified "Royalty-Free" Stock Sites:** Assets from platforms such as YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, or Uppbeat were rejected because their Terms of Service prohibit redistribution of raw audio files within bundled application packages.
4. **Mandatory Explicit Attribution:** Even for CC0 / MIT assets where attribution is not strictly legally punitive, Firefly maintains a dedicated legal attribution registry accessible within the app under `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.

## 3. In-App Legal Disclosure Architecture

The entire legal attribution manifest is packaged locally inside the application bundle at `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. In the Firefly settings and soundscape library views, users can tap **"Third-Party Audio Credits & Open Licenses"** to inspect the source, creator, and license for every sound file, ensuring total transparency.
