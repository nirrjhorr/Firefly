## 1. Evidence Matrix for Curated Collections

The Firefly Audio Sanctuary links soundscape groupings directly to peer-reviewed mechanisms while upholding strict non-curative terminology:

| Collection Name | Evidence Level | Primary Neurobiological / Psychological Mechanism | Key Published Research | Potential Benefits (Cautious Phrasing) | Known Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Natural Hydrology (Rain, Ocean, Stream)** | **Evidence Supported** | Parasympathetic activation via involuntary soft fascination; autonomic recovery. | Kaplan (1995); Ulrich (1991); Buxton et al. (2021) PNAS. | *May support stress reduction and down-regulation of sympathetic nervous system activity.* | Individual differences in sound sensitivity; hyperacusis may alter response. |
| **Broadband Noise (Pink & Brown Noise)** | **Evidence Supported (Pink) / Suggestive (Brown)** | Acoustic masking of abrupt environmental transients; slow-wave sleep entrainment. | Papalambros et al. Neurosci; Stanchina (2005) Sleep Med. | *Associated with fewer nocturnal awakenings and enhanced slow-wave sleep depth in clinical trials.* | Continuous noise at excessive decibels ($>75\,\text{dB}$) can cause auditory fatigue. Psychol. | *Frequently associated with subjective restoration and mood improvement in laboratory and field studies.* | Some individuals with phobias or urban conditioning may experience diminished affinity. |

## 2. In-App User Facing Language Standards

Firefly strictly adheres to the following editorial standards when communicating about audio effects to end users:

```
[AVOID] "This track cures your insomnia."
[USE]   "This track provides steady acoustic masking that may help ease the transition into sleep."

[AVOID] "Listening to this will eliminate depression and panic attacks."
[USE]   "These gentle sounds are commonly used to support moments of calm and emotional grounding."

[AVOID] "Scientific frequencies proven to repair cellular damage."
[USE]   "Curated acoustic environments informed by attention restoration and psychoacoustic research."
```

Every collection in the Firefly UI includes an easily accessible info sheet providing users with the exact evidentiary backing, transparent citations, and empowering them to prioritize their personal acoustic comfort over rigid claims.
